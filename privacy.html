Privacy Policy

Hermes DGX · Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hermes DGX ("the application"), a private single-user automation assistant, handles information it obtains from online accounts that its owner has expressly connected. The application is operated solely by its owner, for the owner's own use.

1. Scope

The application is used exclusively by its operator, on accounts belonging to that operator. It is not distributed, published to any marketplace, or made available to any other person. There are no end users other than the operator.

2. Information the application may access

Only after the operator grants explicit, revocable authorisation, and only for accounts the operator owns, the application may access that operator's own data in the following categories:

  • email messages and their labels;
  • calendar entries;
  • files, documents and spreadsheets;
  • contact records;
  • basic account identity, used solely to identify the authorised account.

3. How information is used

Accessed information is used solely to carry out tasks the operator explicitly requests on the operator's own device. The application does not profile users, does not perform marketing analysis, and does not derive advertising data from this information.

4. Storage and retention

Access credentials are stored on the operator's own device and are never transmitted to any party other than the service the operator connected. Retrieved data is held only in memory or in transient local caches needed to complete a requested task, and is not retained in any external database or cloud service. Credentials and caches can be deleted at any time (see Section 7).

5. Sharing and disclosure

No data is sold, rented, transferred or otherwise shared with any third party. No such data is used to train, fine-tune, or evaluate machine-learning models, and none is made available to advertising platforms. It is not transferred to any party except as required by law. The application does not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

6. Security

All communication with connected services takes place over TLS, and no service other than the one the operator connected ever receives credential material. There is no shared server-side component, and no data belonging to any other person is processed by this application.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

The operator can revoke the application's access at any time from the connected account's own permissions page, which immediately invalidates the stored credentials. Deleting the locally stored credential files removes all data associated with the application.

8. Limited Use disclosure

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

Any change to this policy will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy may be raised through the project page: github.com/snowpeak77/hermes-dgx-oauth.
