Hermes DGX

A private, single-user automation assistant — not a public or commercial service.

What this application is

Hermes DGX is a personal automation agent running on a privately owned workstation. It serves exactly one user: its owner. It is not offered to the public — there is no sign-up, no third-party users, and no multi-tenant service behind it.

Why it requests account access

The agent performs tasks for its owner on accounts the owner owns, using OAuth 2.0 with the owner's explicit consent — for example assembling a daily briefing from the owner's own messages and calendar, or reading and updating a document the owner asks about. Access is limited to what a requested task requires, is revocable at any time, and is never shared with anyone else. Credentials stay on the owner's own device and are never transmitted to any party other than the service the owner connected.

Privacy

No data is collected, aggregated, sold or shared with any third party. See the Privacy Policy for details, including the Limited Use disclosure.
